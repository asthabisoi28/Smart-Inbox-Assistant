I wish to log an adverse drug reaction case for a 58-year-old female patient undergoing therapy with HepaGuard 500mg daily for chronic maintenance.

Baseline liver function tests (LFTs) performed two weeks prior to treatment were within normal limits. Recent routine laboratory monitoring revealed significant elevation of liver enzymes: ALT 340 U/L (normal 7-56) and AST 295 U/L (normal 10-40). The patient also reports mild abdominal right upper quadrant discomfort. No other new medications were initiated. HepaGuard has been discontinued.
