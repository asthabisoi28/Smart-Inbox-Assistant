Dear Safety Team,

I am writing to report a serious adverse reaction observed in a 45-year-old male patient (ID: P-4491) following administration of CardioVax (20mg dose, Lot #CV-9912). 

Approximately 40 minutes post-injection, the patient reported sudden onset of severe dizziness, lightheadedness, and subsequently suffered a brief syncopal episode (fainting) lasting roughly 30 seconds. His blood pressure dropped to 85/55 mmHg. He was stabilized with IV fluids and monitored in our outpatient clinic.
