Dear Drug Safety Officer,

I am submitting an Individual Case Safety Report regarding a 34-year-old male patient who experienced severe acute breathing difficulty (dyspnea) immediately following his first dose of PulmoMist inhaler (100mcg/actuation).

The patient reported tight chest sensation and wheezing within 10 minutes of inhalation. Rescue bronchodilator treatment was administered at our urgent care facility, resulting in gradual symptom resolution. 

Patient Age: 34 | Sex: Male
Suspect Product: PulmoMist (Lot #PM-1182)
Reaction: Acute dyspnea / paradoxical bronchospasm
Outcome: Recovered following emergency intervention
